Hints
  • Protons = Atomic #
  • Neutrons = Mass # − Atomic #
  • Mass # = Protons + Neutrons
  • Charge is like a game score: protons (+) play against electrons (−).
    • More protons than electrons → protons win → positive charge
    • More electrons than protons → electrons win → negative charge
    • Same number → tie → charge is 0 (neutral atom)
    • The charge is how much the winning team wins by. Example: 26 protons vs. 24 electrons → protons win by 2 → +2. 17 protons vs. 18 electrons → electrons win by 1 → −1.
  • Write charges with a sign: +3, −2. A neutral atom has a charge of 0.
  • Element symbols: first letter capital, second letter lowercase (Fe, not FE).
  • In the symbol: mass # goes top left, atomic # bottom left, charge top right. Leave the charge box empty for a neutral atom.
