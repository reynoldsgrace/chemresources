How the numbers fit together
  • Protons = atomic number. The nucleus charge is +protons.
  • In a neutral atom, total electrons = protons.
  • Fill shells from the inside out: 2, then 8, then 8. Leave a shell blank if it has no electrons.
  • Valence electrons are the electrons in the outermost shell.
  • Shielding electrons are the electrons on the inside energy levels.
  • Zeff = protons − shielding electrons.
